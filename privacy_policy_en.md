Last updated: October 9, 2025

---

## 1. Data collected

MetaPano collects the following information during use:

* Discord IDs of users, channels, and guilds.
* The names associated with those IDs.
* Executed commands and their arguments.
* The date and time of execution.

No additional personal data (such as emails or message content) is collected.

---

## 2. Purpose of data collection

Data is collected to:

* Ensure the bot’s **core functionality**.
* Perform **debugging** and **usage analytics**.
* Improve MetaPano’s features over time.

---

## 3. Storage and security

All data is stored locally on Warp’s **personal server located in France**.
Access to this server is **strictly limited** to Warp and protected by secure passwords.

No data is shared with third parties.

---

## 4. Data retention

Collected data is **retained indefinitely**, unless explicitly requested to be deleted by a user.

---

## 5. User rights (GDPR)

In accordance with the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, users may:

* Request access to their data.
* Request correction or deletion of their data.

Requests can be submitted in the **#🔒│requêtes-données** channel on the [Dofus Touls Discord server](https://discord.gg/TcrNrRE5QV).
